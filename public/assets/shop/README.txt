Place your own PNG, WebP, JPG or SVG artwork here.
Then map a stable item ID to its URL in src/shop/artwork.json, for example:
{"sonar":"/assets/shop/sonar.webp", "kraken":"/assets/shop/kraken.png"}
IDs: laugh, salute, oops, luck, storm, gg, sonar, chance, signal, bomb, kraken.
Missing mappings use procedural SVG placeholders; no external assets are requested.
